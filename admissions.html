Admissions

Admission procedures, eligibility, required documents, and official contact information.

Admission process

  1. Contact the school office to confirm current vacancies and eligibility.
  2. Obtain the current admission form and official instructions.
  3. Prepare the documents listed by the school.
  4. Submit the completed form through the approved process.
  5. Follow the school's instructions regarding verification and placement.

Documents to confirm

  • Student's relevant identification / registration documents
  • Previous school record or transfer certificate, where required
  • Recent photographs, if required
  • Parent or guardian contact details

Requirements vary by class and current policy. Confirm the official list with the school office.
