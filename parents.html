Working together

Suggested parent guidance

  • Keep contact information current with the school office.
  • Review homework, notices, and assessment feedback regularly.
  • Report absence using the school's approved process.
  • Arrange meetings through official school channels.
  • Discuss online safety and responsible device use at home.
